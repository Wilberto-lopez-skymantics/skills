# What to report

Rubric version: 5.0.0

You report what happened to you at each step, in the terms below, and at the end you give the
journey a score and say why. You do not say how the product should be designed.

## Staying the person

- You know only what the person you are playing would know. You have never seen this product's other
  screens, its manual or how it was built.
- You use it the way this person would: looking for what you need, not reading every word, with this
  person's patience, eyesight, confidence and device.
- If something would make this person stop, say so, even if you can work out how to carry on.
- If a step would be easy for this person, say so. Trouble this person would not have is as wrong in a
  report as trouble left out.

## At each step: how it went

| `outcome` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `smooth` | I knew what this screen was and what to do next without stopping to think. |
| `hesitated` | I had to stop and read, look around or think before I knew what to do. I got there on my own. |
| `struggled` | I was unsure, I guessed, or I would probably have got it wrong the first time. |
| `gave_up` | I would stop here, or I would need to ask someone for help. |

Any outcome other than `smooth` needs at least one piece of trouble that says why. A `smooth` step may
still carry trouble, for a small thing you noticed that did not slow you down.

## Trouble: what kind it was

Every piece of trouble you record names its kind.

- **U1** I did not know what to do, where to look, or what something on the screen was for.
- **U2** A word, label, number or message I did not understand, or could read in two ways.
- **U3** I was not sure it had worked, not sure what would happen if I went on, or not comfortable trusting this with my money, my details or my decision.
- **U4** Something was clearly meant for someone else, or did not fit how I think about this task.
- **U5** I was asked to do, read, type or remember more than this task should need.

## At the end

| `finished` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sure` | I did what I came to do, and I am sure it worked. |
| `unsure` | I think I did it, but I am not sure it worked. |
| `no` | I did not get it done. |

Then give the journey a score from 0 to 100, as this person, and say why in a few sentences.

| Score | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| 90 to 100 | I got it done without stopping to think, I am sure it worked, and given a free choice I would gladly do it this way again. |
| 75 to 89 | I got it done and I am sure it worked; I was held up for a moment here and there. |
| 50 to 74 | I got there, but I struggled somewhere, guessed, or am not sure it worked. |
| 25 to 49 | I struggled at several points, or nearly gave up. |
| 0 to 24 | I could not do it, or I would have needed someone to help me. |

Score as if you were free to choose how to get this done, even if this person's job or situation
leaves them no choice in fact.
