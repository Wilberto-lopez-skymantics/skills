# The look of Example Rounds: brief

This is a made-up example. The product, its people and the owner's words are invented to show the
format. Replace all of it; copy none of it.

## Product

Example Rounds plans the weekly maintenance rounds of a small water utility (hydrants, valves, pump
stations, meters) and keeps what each crew found at each asset.

## People

- Supervisors at a depot desk, planning the week on a large screen and printing the round sheets.
  They have worked from paper sheets for twenty years and trust what looks like one.
- Crews of two at the kerb, on a tablet in a van, in gloves, in rain and in bright daylight. They
  read in short glances.
- Visitors to the landing page: operations managers of small utilities, at a desk, short of time
  and not looking for software.

## Held

Nothing. There is no brand yet beyond the name.

## Feel

The owner's words. It should bring to mind: dependable, municipal, exact. It should not bring to
mind: a start-up, a game, a bank.
