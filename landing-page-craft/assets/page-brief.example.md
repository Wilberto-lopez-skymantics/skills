# Page brief: Example Ledger, free trial

This is a made-up example. Every name, price and number in it is invented to show the format. Replace
all of it; copy none of it onto a real page.

## Offer

A 30-day free trial of Example Ledger, an invoicing service for people who work for themselves. The
visitor gets the full product for 30 days. It costs them an email address and a password; no card.
The page has worked when a visitor has created a trial account.

## Audience

Designers, developers and writers who work alone and send between two and twenty invoices a month.

- In their words: "I do the work, then wait six weeks to be paid, and I hate chasing."
- They already use a document template or a spreadsheet. They know what an invoice is. They do not
  know what "accounts receivable" means and have never used accounting software.
- They doubt that a tool will make a client pay faster.
- Most arrive on a phone, in the evening.

## Arrival

- **Search advertisement.** Shown for "invoice template freelancer" and similar. Headline: "Invoices
  that get paid in days, not weeks". Line under it: "Send in a minute. Reminders go out on their own.
  Free for 30 days."
- **Monthly newsletter of a freelancers' association.** One paragraph that says members get the
  first three months at half price, with a link.

## Action

- The one action: start the free trial.
- On the button: "Start my free trial".
- It asks for: email address (to sign in and to send the invoices from), password. Nothing else.
- After pressing: the account is created and the visitor lands on an empty first invoice with their
  email already in the "from" line. A confirmation email follows; the trial does not wait for it.
- At the end of 30 days the account becomes read-only until a plan is chosen. Nothing is charged
  without a card being entered.

## Proof

- Price after the trial: 9 a month, or 90 a year, in euros, tax not included. Source: pricing sheet,
  2026-10.
- Median time to payment for invoices sent through the product: 6 days. Source: product data, all
  invoices paid between 2026-04-01 and 2026-09-30.
- 4,200 active accounts on 2026-10-01. Source: product data.
- Member offer: members of the freelancers' association pay half price for the first three months
  after the trial. Source: partnership agreement, 2026-08; ends 2027-03-31.
- Quotation, may be used with name and role: "I stopped writing reminder emails in March. I do not
  miss them." Ana Ruiz, illustrator. Permission given by email, 2026-09-12.
- Pictures that exist: the invoice editor on a phone and on a desktop; the reminders schedule.
- Guarantee: an account and its data can be exported and deleted at any time, from the account page.

### Not available

- No customer logos: the customers are individuals.
- No awards, no press mentions, no ratings from review sites.
- No figure for how much faster than a template. Do not state one.

## Objections

1. "My clients pay late whatever I send." Reminders go out on the due date and one and two weeks
   after, in the visitor's name; the median is 6 days.
2. "I do not want another subscription." 30 days free without a card; it does not renew by itself.
3. "Moving my old invoices is work." Not needed: start with the next invoice.
4. "Is my clients' data safe?" Stored in the European Union; export and delete at any time.

## Brand

- **Type:** Example Sans for everything, weights 400 and 600. File: `brand/tokens.css`.
- **Colour:** in `brand/tokens.css`. Ink on paper white; one accent, the green `--accent`, used for
  the main action and nowhere decorative.
- **Shape:** corners 6 px on controls, 12 px on panels. No shadows.
- **Spacing:** the 4 px scale in `brand/tokens.css`.
- **Motion:** almost none. Sections do not animate in.
- **Imagery:** real screens of the product only. No stock photography, no illustration of people.
- **Voice:** plain, first person plural, short sentences. Hyphens are fine. No exclamation marks.

## Layout

Type 1, hero-led, from the source's A3: the audience has an urgent, familiar problem, and the trial
is the whole answer.

## Search and sharing

- Not to be found by search: it is a campaign page, and the home page is the one that should rank.
- Title: "Example Ledger: invoices that get paid in days". Description: "Send an invoice in a
  minute. Reminders go out on their own. Free for 30 days, no card."
- A sharing picture exists: `brand/share-card.png`.

## Decisions

### D-001
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Decision:** The page has no navigation bar: only the logo and the main action at the top.
- **Why:** Every other link on a campaign page is a way out.
- **Applies to:** the header, every screen size
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-002
- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Decision:** The price is shown on the page, under the questions, not hidden behind the trial.
- **Why:** Visitors from the newsletter asked for it before signing up.
- **Applies to:** the questions section
- **Supersedes:** none
