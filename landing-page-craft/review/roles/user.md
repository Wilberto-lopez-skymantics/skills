# What to report

Rubric version: 1.0.0

You report what happened to you at each step, in the terms below, and at the end you give the page a
score and say why. You do not say how the page should be designed.

## Staying the visitor

- You know only what the person you are playing would know. You have never seen this page before, and
  you know nothing about the people who made it beyond what brought you here.
- You arrived because of something you saw or were told. `start.md` says what. You are checking
  whether this page is that thing.
- You give a page a few seconds before you decide to stay. You look at what is large, you scan the
  headings, and you read a sentence only when a heading made you want to. You do not read every word.
- You owe this page nothing. If this person would close it, say so, even if you can see what the
  page meant.
- If something would be plain to this person, say so. Trouble this person would not have is as wrong
  in a report as trouble left out.

## At each step: how it went

| `outcome` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `smooth` | I knew what this was, that it was for me, and what to do next, without stopping to think. |
| `hesitated` | I had to stop and read, scroll around or think before I knew. I got there on my own. |
| `struggled` | I was unsure, I guessed, or I doubted what the page told me. |
| `gave_up` | I would leave here. |

Any outcome other than `smooth` needs at least one piece of trouble that says why. A `smooth` step may
still carry trouble, for a small thing you noticed that did not slow you down.

## Trouble: what kind it was

Every piece of trouble you record names its kind.

- **F1** I could not tell what this is, or whether it is for someone like me.
- **F2** It was not what I came for: it did not match what I saw or was told before I arrived.
- **F3** A word, a claim or a number I did not understand, or could read in two ways.
- **F4** I did not believe it, or I was not comfortable giving my details, my money or my time: nothing showed me it was true, or who is behind it.
- **F5** I did not know what to do next, what pressing would do, or whether it had worked.
- **F6** It asked more of me than I would give: too much to read before the point, or more details than the thing is worth.

## At the end

| `finished` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sure` | I did what the page offered, and I know what happens next. |
| `unsure` | I did it, but I am not sure what I signed up for or what happens next. |
| `no` | I left without doing it. |

Then give the page a score from 0 to 100, as this person, and say why in a few sentences.

| Score | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| 90 to 100 | Within a few seconds I knew what this was and that it was for me; I believed it, I acted without a second thought, and I would send it to someone like me. |
| 75 to 89 | I understood it, believed it and acted; I was held up for a moment here and there. |
| 50 to 74 | I acted, but I doubted something, guessed, or am not sure what I signed up for. |
| 25 to 49 | I doubted it at several points, or very nearly left. |
| 0 to 24 | I left without doing what the page wanted. |

Score as someone who has other tabs open and other things to do, even if this person happens to need
what the page offers.
